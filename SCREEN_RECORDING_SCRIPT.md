# Screen Recording Script: Click-by-Click Guide

*Ensure you have completed `DEMO_CHECKLIST.md` before beginning.*

**Scene 1: System Entry**
1. Start recording on `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Mouse over the portfolio balance.
3. Click the "Load Evaluation Portfolio ($10,420)" button to populate the screen with sample holdings.
4. Pause for 3 seconds to let the user absorb the Dashboard.

**Scene 2: Market Discovery**
5. Click "Live Markets" in the top navigation (or the "View Live PreStocks Markets" button).
6. Smoothly scroll down the page to show the Pyth and PreStocks live data feeds.
7. Stop scrolling when `SPACEX` is clearly visible on the screen.

**Scene 3: Identifying the Divergence**
8. Click on the `SPACEX` asset row to navigate to `/assets/SPACEX`.
9. Mouse over the "Onchain price" and "Reference price (Fair Value)" cards to highlight the difference.
10. Mouse over the "Best Execution Route" Jupiter comparison if available.

**Scene 4: AI Analysis**
11. Click the "Market analysis ->" button at the bottom of the page.
12. Pause for 3 seconds on the `/assets/SPACEX/analysis` page.
13. Mouse over the bolded "Primary risk" text generated by the AI.

**Scene 5: Deterministic Risk Governor**
14. Click the "Review action ->" button.
15. You are now on `/assets/SPACEX/action`.
16. Mouse over the "Policy check" grid, specifically highlighting the "Exposure now", "Exposure after", and "Result" rows.

**Scene 6: Execution**
17. Click the "🔍 Solana Instruction Payload Inspector" toggle to expand it.
18. Briefly mouse over the `Instruction 2: SPL Memo Risk Attestation` block.
19. Click the green "Sign & execute on-chain" button.
20. When the Phantom/Solflare wallet extension pops up, quickly click "Confirm/Approve".

**Scene 7: On-chain Proof**
21. Wait for the success UI to render on the page.
22. Click the "View on Solscan ->" link.
23. On the Solscan page, scroll down to the "Instructions" tab.
24. Expand the SPL Memo instruction to show the plain text: `AfterHours: BUY $X SPACEX | Risk Governor: Passed...`
25. Finally, navigate to `http://localhost:3000/proof` to show the transparent verification dashboard.
26. Stop recording.
